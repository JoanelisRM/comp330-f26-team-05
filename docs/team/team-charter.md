## Communication

**Communications Channels:** We will use Microsoft Teams as our main channel for day to day discussion, questions and coordination. And Github Issue and pull request comments should be used for anything specifics related work, so that decision and it's time chain can stay in record.

**Response Time:** Teammates should expect a reply within 24 hours on any given day. If someone is unavailable for more than 24 hours, they should inform the team in advance in Microsoft Teams.

**Urgent Issues:** Because some members have found Teams unreliable, urgent issues should not be relied upon Teams alone. It should be sent through a backup channel (text or email) which is to be decided by the team. Reply time for urgent issues is expected to be within few hours.

## Participation and Accountability

**Reasonable Participation** Full expectations will be decided and confirmed at our first team meeting. But at minimum, every member is expected to respond to team messages within the agreed response window, and is mandatory to attend scheduled meetings, if they cannot attend due unforeseen circumstances they should notify the team via communication channels. Every member is expected to contribute to each assignment.

**Problem with Meeting a Commitment** If there is an issue in fulfilling a commitment the member should notify the team as early as possible, ideally before the deadline, they should explain what they can still complete and what needs to be done by their backup as everyone has one backup available.

**Unresponsiveness** If a member does not respond for 72 hours during active assignment, the team should try following up through the backup urgent channel. If the problem persists, the team should discuss with the member to resolve the issue and, if needed, raise it with the instructor.

## AI and Engineering Responsibility

**AI Use Responsibility** Our team will use AI assisted tools mentioned in AI policy for assistants in brainstorming, drafting, debugging, testing ideas, and for reviewing work. AI work should be treated as a draft and is subject to a final review by a team member. The team is fully responsible for everything committed to the repository. It's strictly advised that no member should submit any AI assisted work they cannot defend.

**AI Log** Significant AI use should be recorded in `/docs/ai/ai-use-log.md`, and detailed vefrification evidence should be recorded in `/docs/ai/ai-verification-notes.md` when needed.
